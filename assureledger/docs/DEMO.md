# Seven-minute recruiter demonstration

1. **0:00–0:45 — Business problem.** Explain the fictional SaaS boundary and why weak evidence can mislead management.
2. **0:45–2:00 — Risk.** Trace R03 from data-loss exposure to treatment ownership. Explain why 20 → 10 is a forecast, not a proven reduction.
3. **2:00–3:00 — SoA.** Show why controls were selected and why the pilot is incomplete. Challenge the illustrative physical-entry exclusion.
4. **3:00–4:00 — Evidence.** Open the expired restore record and the rejected closure. Explain freshness, scope and independent review.
5. **4:00–5:00 — Automation.** Run the CLI and tests. Change a COPY of a fixture to show integrity failure. Do not alter the original demo invisibly.
6. **5:00–6:00 — Management communication.** Explain the three decisions requested in the audit pack and their business impact.
7. **6:00–7:00 — Limitations.** Explain the absence of authenticated approvals and what would be required for production.

## Personal contribution exercise
Before putting this on your CV: rewrite one risk narrative in your own words, independently justify the scores, review a mapping against the standard available through university access, add one test, and record your conclusions in a pull request. Retain evidence of your own analysis. AI-assisted scaffolding is not a substitute for understanding.

## CV wording after personal verification
“Developed and validated an AI-assisted ISO/IEC 27001-oriented GRC portfolio prototype linking risk assessments, pilot applicability decisions, evidence freshness checks and corrective-action reporting using synthetic SaaS scenarios.”

Do not claim certification, production deployment, real-client auditing, job experience or full standard coverage. A credible project can strengthen applications but cannot guarantee employment.

## Interview questions to prepare
Why is an accepted document insufficient to prove effectiveness? What makes evidence relevant? Who can accept residual risk? How do you justify exclusions? Why does one passing technical check not make an ISO control compliant? How would you audit the tool itself?
