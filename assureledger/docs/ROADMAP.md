## Prioritized backlog
P0: publication; personal review; full requirement coverage validation. P1: evidence metadata schema, decision history, real lab observations, acceptance workflow. P2: database/API and authenticated UI only after an explicit threat model. P3: carefully reviewed framework mappings and AI supplier risk extension. Do not claim AI governance coverage from an ISO 27001 mapping alone.

## Definition of done for a feature
A business need, an observable decision consequence, tests for incorrect or missing evidence, documented limitations, a reproducible demo and no sensitive data in the repository. Human approvals must never be fabricated by a generator.
