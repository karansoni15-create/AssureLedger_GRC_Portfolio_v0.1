# Eight-minute recruiter demonstration

1. **0:00–0:45 — Business problem.** Explain the fictional SaaS boundary and why weak evidence can mislead management.
2. **0:45–2:00 — Risk.** Trace R03 from data-loss exposure to treatment ownership. Explain why 20 → 10 is a forecast, not a proven reduction.
3. **2:00–3:00 — SoA.** Open `soa.csv`: 93 rows, 19 decided. Explain why the tool refuses to call it complete and challenge the illustrative physical-entry exclusion.
4. **3:00–4:00 — Evidence.** Open the expired restore record and the rejected closure. Explain freshness, scope and independent review.
5. **4:00–5:00 — Automation.** Run the CLI and tests. Change a COPY of a fixture to show integrity failure. Do not alter the original demo invisibly.
6. **5:00–6:00 — Acceptance authority.** Show R06: residual 9 is above the owner's limit, so the managing director signed. Change the signer to the IT lead in a copy and show `exceeds_authority`.
7. **6:00–7:00 — Obligations and management.** Walk through OB02 (30-day breach assessment) and OB03 (72-hour ransomware payment report), then the three decisions requested in the audit pack.
8. **7:00–8:00 — Limitations.** Explain the absence of authenticated approvals and what would be required for production.

## Personal contribution exercise
Before putting this on your CV: rewrite one risk narrative in your own words, independently justify the scores, review a mapping against the standard available through university access, add one test, and record your conclusions in a pull request. Retain evidence of your own analysis. AI-assisted scaffolding is not a substitute for understanding.

## CV wording after personal verification
“Built and tested an AI-assisted ISO/IEC 27001:2022-oriented GRC workbench (Python, 57 automated tests) linking a risk register and treatment plan, residual-risk acceptance authority, a 93-control Statement of Applicability, evidence freshness checks and an Australian obligations register (Privacy Act, NDB scheme, Cyber Security Act 2024) for a synthetic SaaS company.”

Do not claim certification, production deployment, real-client auditing, job experience or full standard coverage. A credible project can strengthen applications but cannot guarantee employment.

## Interview questions to prepare
Why is an accepted document insufficient to prove effectiveness? What makes evidence relevant? Who can accept residual risk? How do you justify exclusions? Why does one passing technical check not make an ISO control compliant? How would you audit the tool itself? Why can the IT lead not accept R06? What changes for an Australian organisation now that ASD Essentials has replaced the Essential Eight? Which of OB01–OB03 would you confirm with legal first, and why?
